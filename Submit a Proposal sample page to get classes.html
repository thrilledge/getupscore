Virtual Assistant Needed for Beauty Business & Podcast Brand (Website, Google, Social Media & Admin)

  • General Virtual Assistance
  • Posted Aug 3, 2026
I'm looking for a reliable, proactive, and detail-oriented Virtual Assistant to become a long-term part of my team. I own a beauty business specializing in waxing, ingrown hair treatments, and skincare, and I'm also growing a podcast and educational brand. I'm looking for someone who can help keep everything organized, consistent, and growing. ## Responsibilities * Manage and update my website (WordPress, Wix, or similar platforms) * Create and schedule Google Business Profile posts * Manage and optimize Google Ads campaigns * Schedule and publish social media content across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and other platforms * Maintain my content calendar * Schedule podcast episodes and promotional content * Upload blog posts, videos, and promotional content * Monitor website updates and ensure everything is working properly * Manage my business calendar and appointments * Respond to basic emails and messages when needed * Research marketing ideas, trends, and competitors * Assist with other administrative tasks as the business grows ## Ideal Candidate * Excellent English communication skills * Proven experience managing websites and social media * Experience with Google Ads and Google Business Profile * Familiar with Canva, ChatGPT, and AI productivity tools * Knowledge of SEO is a bonus * Experience using scheduling platforms such as Buffer, Later, Metricool, or Hootsuite * Organized, dependable, and able to work independently * Strong attention to detail and ability to meet deadlines ## Bonus Skills * Beauty, wellness, or skincare industry experience * Podcast production or podcast marketing experience * Basic graphic design * Video editing using CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, or similar software * Email marketing experience with Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or similar platforms I'm looking for someone who takes initiative, communicates well, and enjoys helping a growing business succeed. I value honesty, creativity, and someone who can think ahead rather than waiting for instructions on every task. This is a long-term opportunity for the right person, with room for increased hours and responsibilities as the business grows. **To apply, please include:** 1. A brief introduction about yourself. 2. Links to websites or social media accounts you've managed. 3. Your experience with Google Ads and Google Business Profile. 4. Your experience with podcast or content scheduling (if applicable). 5. Your experience with salon / spa scheduling & requirements 6. Your hourly rate. 7. Your availability (hours per week and time zone). **Please start your proposal with the words "Sugar Island" so I know you've read the entire posting.**
