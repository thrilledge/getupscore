Frequently asked questions

Is the Free plan really free forever? expand_more

Yes. The Free plan gives you access to recent jobs, standard opportunity scores, and our basic extension without asking for credit card information.

How fast are the job alerts dispatched? expand_more

Within 60 to 180 seconds of a client hitting publish on Upwork. Our distributed worker network crawls feeds around the clock with zero lag.

Does UpScore require my Upwork login credentials? expand_more

Never. We will never ask for your password or direct account credentials. Our ingestion engine monitors public feed APIs and RSS infrastructure.

Which notification destinations are supported? expand_more

You can push leads straight to Telegram, Slack, custom HTTPS webhooks, WhatsApp, Gmail, Make, n8n, and Zapier.

How does the 0-100 UpScore metric work? expand_more

Our model evaluates the client's past hire percentage, average rate paid, feedback score, job description specificity, and current proposal count.
